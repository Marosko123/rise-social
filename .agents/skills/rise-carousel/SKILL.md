# Rise carousel

Build text-first carousels with authentic supporting media.

## Inputs

Approved brief, claims, selected assets/rights, platform, visual direction and rendered preview.

## Contract

- 4–8 slides, 1080×1350, one idea per slide.
- Cover gives a concrete promise without clickbait: maximum seven words, two rendered lines,
  approximately 80–96 px. Content headlines are 54–64 px, body 34–40 px, at most 45 words.
- Use an 84 px safe margin, AA contrast, restrained type and slide numbering.
- LinkedIn PDF pages are flattened and exactly match the Instagram page size.
- Every Instagram grid tile works independently; never build a puzzle grid.
- Prefer product screenshots, UI details, team photos and branded diagrams.
- Write alt text for the information conveyed, not decorative styling.

Choose one master narrative: Product Anatomy (6 slides), Decision Note (7), Before/After (6),
or Signal vs. Noise (6). Vary whole product, detail, diagram and calm text; never repeat a
laptop mockup as every page.

## Output contract

Return template, slide order, dimensions, safe zones, asset IDs/crops, claim mapping and alt text.

Inspect overflow at desktop and mobile review widths before approval.

## Stop conditions

Stop on cropped text, unreadable contrast, missing alt text, uncertain image rights,
generic stock decoration or generative imagery without explicit approval.

## Pressure test

Good: a six-slide Product Anatomy alternates UI, flow, diagram and decision. Bad: a three-line
cover or six generic notebook frames fails visual QA.
