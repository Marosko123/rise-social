# Rise social continuity

Load imported public history and recent local runs before drafting.

## Workflow

1. Compare the concept, title and first eight meaningful words with prior posts.
2. Compare the promised takeaway and examples, not only exact wording.
3. Use negative-space analysis: identify what the history has not explained concretely.
4. Connect to relevant earlier content only when the new post adds evidence or a new decision.
5. Record the compared prior-post IDs in the draft.

## Output contract

Return compared history IDs and a pass/fail result for topic, title, opening and promised
takeaway. Campaign siblings additionally need distinct opening, promise and core.

## Stop conditions

Stop when history is unavailable but the draft claims novelty, or when the new post
only paraphrases an existing opening, carousel or unsupported claim.

## Pressure test

Good: a later map post explains a new buyer decision with another proof type. Bad: two campaign
siblings that promise the same takeaway are reduced to one master post.
